# Requirements

## Functional

- Identify candidate industrial pathways for spider-silk-like materials.
- Quantify key mechanical and process metrics where evidence exists.
- Separate facts, hypotheses, and assumptions explicitly.

## Non-functional

- Every material-property claim must include conditions when known
  (temperature, humidity, strain rate, sample geometry).
- No claim may be promoted from ASSUMPTION to FACT without independent evidence.

## Out of scope (MVP scaffold)

- Full techno-economic analysis
- Lab experimental execution
- Regulatory dossier
