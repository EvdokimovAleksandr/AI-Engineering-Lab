# Assumptions

> Kind: ASSUMPTION — do not treat as FACT.

1. "Spider silk" here means dragline / major ampullate silk mechanical class unless stated otherwise.
2. Industrial scale means continuous or batch production beyond laboratory grams/day.
3. Published tensile strengths often come from controlled lab conditions and may not transfer to scaled fiber.
4. Biological host expression and synthetic spinning are both in scope as competing routes.

Update this file only via the lab artifact/decision process when assumptions change.
